# Same-brand coinciding emergency drops

2026-11-19. Inside the existing collapsed Overview emergency strip, related alerts are summarized by authorized brand, platform and identical reporting day. No new requests, database reads, collection, timers, Telegram messages or detection thresholds are introduced. Individual alerts and measurements remain available below. Brand/platform filters apply to group summaries too; expansion uses the existing in-memory disclosure mechanism and clears on logout.

Policy: at least two distinct canonical branches must have confirmed EMERGENCY states, and represent at least half of same-day assessed (NORMAL or EMERGENCY) branches with valid orders and baseline >=4.5/day. Missing evidence, insufficient baselines, low volume, other reporting dates and duplicate branch/platform identities are excluded and their authorized source-row count is disclosed. Legacy rows without canonical branch identity cannot form groups. The server projects canonical branch identity only after current active branch, brand, feature and source scoping.

Shared signals require the same code in each branch's date/baseline-matched diagnosis, at least two affected branches and at least half of the affected cohort. Primary/supporting duplicates count once per branch. Each branch retains its own frozen pre-incident baseline; different baselines are not pooled. Signals are associated investigation clues, not shared causes. Concurrent drops do not imply concurrent onset; actual incident start dates remain visible.

Other-platform evidence is mentioned only for the same canonical affected branch, brand and identical reporting day among authorized unique source rows with confirmed emergencies. Absence is explicitly not proof that a problem is platform-exclusive. No cross-platform sales, orders, ratings or percentages are combined.

The feature groups urgent **order** drops using the existing >=80% policy; it does not claim to detect every sales decline or menu failure. Repair suggestions are checks, not findings. Coverage and individual alerts remain authoritative.
